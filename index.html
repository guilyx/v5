2014–2021Nantes · Bordeaux, France

Electronics first, then robots

A B.Sc. in electronics engineering at Nantes Université, then two master's degrees: computer engineering and aeronautics at Ynov Bordeaux, and control and robotics at Ecole Centrale de Nantes.

2018–2019Hiventive · Bordeaux, France

Embedded Software Engineer

Starting at the chip

I built a virtual prototype of an STM32 microcontroller in SystemC/TLM, with its timers, I2C, CAN, GPIO and USART peripherals, running inside QEMU.

The lesson I kept: treat time, memory and bandwidth as a budget, and test against a model of the hardware first.

  • QEMU integration

2021–2022Coalescent Mobile Robotics · Odense, Denmark

Founding Robotics Engineer

A robot in a real supermarket

Part of the founding technical team that delivered the first working in-store robot. I led the navigation stack (path planning, motion control, localization) and worked on perception: detection, filtering, tracking.

After the CTO left, I led navigation and trolley detection: I recruited and trained interns and a junior engineer, ran planning, and turned the CEO's requirements into sprints.

2024–2026Technology Innovation Institute · Abu Dhabi

Team Lead, Robotics Software

Leading the team

I grew and led a team of 9 engineers across autonomy, infrastructure and platform integration. We overhauled the swarm autonomy architecture.

A joint program with Caltech on M4, their multi-modal robot that flies and rolls, and on their humanoid, including outdoor planning and tracking for M4. Demos to companies in Europe and the UAE led to more than USD 10M in contracts. I also started building AI tooling for the team.

2024–nowUnchained Labs · remote

Founder

Say it, and it gets built

Local-first AI agents that run on a company's own models, data and infrastructure. Kymatics is graph-based agent orchestration: a spoken intent becomes a queue of build jobs.

How I use AI in engineering: it lets a team ship more without lowering the bar, and the real work is the harness around it. Every change goes draft → AI review → checks → quiz (“can you explain your diff?”) → human review → ship, whoever or whatever wrote it.

unchainedlabs.dev GitHub

  • Local-first agents

Feb 2026–nowSIRB.AI · Abu Dhabi

Head of Robotics

سرب, a flock

Founding team. I designed the robotics software architecture and the autonomy roadmap, and built a self-healing autonomy layer: swarm-wide fault and state recording, agents that detect and recover from failures, ML-based mission scoring before launch.

We took the stack from prototype to public launch at UMEX 2026, where it was unveiled as the Middle East's first AI-powered swarm platform. Sirb is Arabic for a flock, or a swarm.
