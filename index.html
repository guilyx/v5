Things I build

Tools for robots, planners, and the agents around them. Each one comes with a short clip.

grip

A git hook that quizzes you on your own diff before you commit or push, grades the answers against a hidden rubric, and blocks anything below the pass mark.

PyMAPF

Multi-agent pathfinding on arbitrary graphs: CBS, PIBT, LaCAM, MAPF-LNS, plus multi-agent RL. The solvers stream their search live.

CodeSite

Kymatics Unchained Labs

Turns a spoken intent into a queue of build jobs, with local-first agents.

CodeSite

flybots

Multirotor, fixed-wing and VTOL flight physics from scratch, 40+ runnable simulations, and an RL gym.

CodeSite

rostree

Explores a ROS 2 dependency graph (a DAG, not a tree) from the CLI, a TUI, or a self-contained HTML file.

CodeSite

PythonPDDL

CodeSite
