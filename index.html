The journey

Scroll, and the swarm draws each chapter.

2018 Hiventive · Bordeaux

Starting at the chip

I wrote a virtual STM32 microcontroller in SystemC: timers, I2C, CAN, GPIO and USART, all running under QEMU. Testing against a model of the hardware first became a habit I never dropped.

  • SystemC virtual prototype
  • QEMU integration

Formation 01 — the chip

2019 Ingeniarius · Coimbra

First robots, first wins

Full autonomy stacks with ROS on small robots, built from scratch. They won both maze competitions: the one with a map, and the one without.

  • 2 of 2 maze competitions won

Formation 02 — a maze, and the path through it

2020 Centrale Nantes

Planning, and two papers

Four research projects: multi-agent pathfinding, PDDL planning, real-time ROS on Xenomai, and ROS 2 latency. I've had a soft spot for planning problems ever since.

  • Paper Survey of multi-agent pathfinding
  • Paper Real-time jitter under ROS 2

Formation 03 — three agents sharing a grid, no collisions

2021 Coalescent · Odense

A robot in a real supermarket

I built the navigation for a robot working store aisles: path planning, motion control and localization. When the CTO left, I took over navigation and trolley detection, hired our first interns, and ran the sprints.

  • Founding robotics engineer
  • First team I led

Formation 04 — the other thing in the aisle

2022 Technology Innovation Institute · Abu Dhabi

From one robot to many

Multi-robot orchestration and new swarm behaviours, including a decentralized, bird-inspired flocking method we published at IROS. The live demos brought in USD 3M in contracts.

  • IROS 2024 Bird-inspired flocking
  • $3M in contracts

Formation 05 — where the flocking idea came from

2024 Technology Innovation Institute · Abu Dhabi

Leading the team

I grew and led a team of nine across autonomy, infrastructure and integration. We overhauled the swarm stack, ran a joint program with Caltech, and our demos led to more than USD 10M in contracts.

  • −74% onboard CPU
  • −52% bandwidth
  • 9 engineers

Formation 06 — the CPU we gave back to the drones

2024 Unchained Labs

Starting a company

AI agents that run on a company's own models, data and machines, and Kymatics, a graph-based orchestrator that turns a spoken request into a queue of jobs.

  • unchainedlabs.dev
  • Local-first agents
