  2. Act I 2018–2019 · Bordeaux, France

    Starting at the chip

    Hiventive · Embedded Software Engineer

    I built a SystemC/TLM virtual prototype of an STM32 microcontroller (timers, I2C, CAN, GPIO, USART) and integrated it with QEMU.

    What stuck: treat time, memory and bandwidth as a budget, and test against a model of the hardware first.

    In the sky: the microcontroller, signals running in.

  3. Act II 2019 · Coimbra, Portugal

    Two mazes, two wins

    Ingeniarius · Robotics Assistant

    Full autonomy stacks with ROS and embedded systems, built from scratch. They won both maze-solving competitions: the mapped one and the unmapped one.

    • 2 of 2 competitions won

    In the sky: a maze, and the path through it.

  4. Act III 2020–2021 · Nantes, France

    Research, in real time

    Ecole Centrale de Nantes · Robotics Researcher

    I led four research projects: multi-agent pathfinding, AI planning in PDDL, real-time ROS control on Xenomai, and ROS 2 latency benchmarking. On the side: Go backend services for Hiventive, remotely.

    • Paper Jitter under ROS 2
    • Paper Multi-agent pathfinding survey

    In the sky: the inverted pendulum from the jitter paper, balancing.

  5. Act IV 2021–2022 · Odense, Denmark

    A robot in a real supermarket

    Coalescent Mobile Robotics · Founding Robotics Engineer

    Our founding team delivered the first working in-store robot. I led navigation (planning, control, localization), then trolley detection too after the CTO left, with interns and a junior engineer I recruited and trained.

    “A real store changes your priorities fast.”

    In the sky: a trolley, detected.

  6. Act V 2022–2024 · Abu Dhabi

    Learning from birds

    Technology Innovation Institute · Senior Engineer, Robotics Software

    R&D for multi-robot orchestration, collaboration and control. I designed new swarm behaviours, including decentralized bird-inspired flocking, published at IEEE/RSJ IROS 2024. The live demos led to USD 3M in contracts.

    • IROS 2024 Bird-inspired flocking
    • $3M in contracts

    In the sky: a flock, each drone steering by its neighbours. Move your cursor through it, or tap.

  7. Act VI 2024–2026 · Abu Dhabi

    A lighter swarm

    Technology Innovation Institute · Team Lead, Robotics Software

    I grew and led a team of 9 across autonomy, infrastructure and integration, and we overhauled the swarm autonomy architecture. With Caltech: outdoor planning and tracking for M4, their flying-and-rolling robot. Demos in Europe and the UAE led to USD 10M+ in contracts.

    • −74% onboard CPU
    • −52% bandwidth
    • 9 engineers

    In the sky: the CPU we gave back to every drone.

  8. Act VII 2024–now · remote

    A voice becomes a queue of jobs

    Unchained Labs · Founder

    Local-first AI agents that run on a company's own models, data and infrastructure. Kymatics, our graph-based orchestrator, turns a spoken intent into a queue of build jobs.

    • unchainedlabs.dev

    In the sky: a voice, a graph of agents, a queue of jobs.

  9. Act VIII Feb 2026–now · Abu Dhabi

    Head of Robotics, SIRB.AI

    Founding team · سرب, sirb, is Arabic for a swarm

    I designed the robotics architecture and a self-healing autonomy layer: swarm-wide fault and state recording, agents that detect and recover from failures, ML-based mission scoring before launch.

    • UMEX 2026 public launch

    In the sky: watch for a drone turning red. It fails, it's caught, it recovers.
