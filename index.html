    Things I build

    Eight projects, each with a short clip. Every change goes draft → AI review → checks → quiz → human review → ship, whoever or whatever wrote it. grip is that quiz, as a hook.

    Bay 01

    grip

    A git hook that quizzes you on your own diff before you commit or push, grades the answers against a hidden rubric, and blocks anything below the pass mark.

    CodeSite

    Bay 02

    flybots

    Multirotor, fixed-wing and VTOL flight physics from scratch: 40+ runnable simulations and an RL gym.

    CodeSite

    Bay 03

    PyMAPF

    Multi-agent pathfinding on arbitrary graphs: CBS, PIBT, LaCAM, MAPF-LNS and multi-agent RL. The solvers stream their search live.

    CodeSite

    Bay 04

    Kymatics Unchained Labs

    Graph-based agent orchestration: a spoken intent becomes a queue of build jobs, run by local-first agents.

    CodeUnchained Labs

    Bay 05

    rostree

    Explores a ROS 2 dependency graph (a DAG, not a tree) from the CLI, a TUI, or a self-contained HTML file.

    CodeSite

    Bay 06

    PythonPDDL

    A dependency-free PDDL framework: hand-written parser and grounder, 14 planners, LM-cut and FF heuristics, and heuristics you can train.

    CodeSite

    Bay 07

    roue

    An offline file converter for the Linux desktop: Ctrl+Shift+right-click a file, pick a format on a wheel, and the result lands next to the original.

    CodeSite

    Bay 08

    Clanker Labs

    Self-host your home (notes, mail, media, passwords, money) on hardware you own, with an agent in front so it doesn't need a sysadmin.
