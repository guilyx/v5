I lead robotics software teams across orchestration, navigation and fault management. Eight years in robotics, leading teams since 2021, and still hands-on in ROS 2, C++, Python and Go (some Rust and TypeScript too).

This site is a maze, and the robot is yours.

It's a toy M4, the robot from Caltech that rolls and flies (I worked on its outdoor planning).

  • Point at the floor to preview a plan, click to go. The planner can roll, fly over walls, or morph between the two, trading energy for time. A ghost rehearses the route first.
  • F folds the wheels into rotors and takes off; F again lands. C shows the local costmap.
  • Scroll, or press Solve. It visits every room in order: one room per chapter.
  • Arrow keys or WASD drive it by hand, on the ground or in the air.
