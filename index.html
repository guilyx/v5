A microcontroller, in software

As an embedded software engineer, I built a SystemC/TLM virtual prototype of an STM32: TIM, I2C, CAN, GPIO and USART, wired into QEMU. Same years: an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering & Aeronautics at Ynov Bordeaux.

05 2021–2022 Coalescent Mobile Robotics · Odense

A robot in a real supermarket

Founding robotics engineer on the team that delivered the first working in-store robot. I led navigation: planning, motion control, localization. When the CTO left, I also took on trolley detection, trained interns and a junior engineer, and ran the sprints.

A real store changes your priorities fast.

06 2022–2026 Technology Innovation Institute · Abu Dhabi

From one robot to a swarm

I designed swarm behaviours, including bird-inspired flocking published at IROS 2024, then grew and led a team of 9. We cut onboard CPU by 74% and bandwidth by 52%, and worked with Caltech on their M4 robot. Demos led to USD 3M, then over USD 10M, in contracts.

  • IROS 2024 Bird-inspired flocking
  • −74% onboard CPU
  • 9 engineers

On the shelf: a drone, and the arm lets it fly

07 2024–now Unchained Labs · remote

Agents that stay in-house

I founded Unchained Labs: local-first AI agents that run on a company's own models, data and infrastructure. Kymatics, our graph-based orchestrator, turns a spoken intent into a queue of build jobs.

  • unchainedlabs.dev
  • Founder

On the shelf: a microphone and its sound wave

08 Feb 2026–now SIRB.AI · Abu Dhabi

A swarm that heals itself

Head of Robotics, founding team. I designed the robotics architecture and a self-healing autonomy layer: fault and state recording across the swarm, agents that detect and recover from failures, ML-based mission scoring before launch. We went from prototype to public launch at UMEX 2026.
